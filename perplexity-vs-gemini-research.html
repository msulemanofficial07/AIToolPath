Perplexity vs Gemini for Research and Recent Information

My starting recommendation

I try Perplexity first when I want a conversational web-search workflow and visible source leads. I try Gemini when Google-connected workflows, multimodal input, or features in my account fit the assignment. In either case, the answer is a research starting point: I open and read the evidence.

My source-checking routine

  1. Separate factual questions, interpretation, and opinion.
  2. Open the original source instead of citing a generated summary.
  3. Check author, date, evidence, and whether it supports the exact claim.
  4. For an important point, find another authoritative source.

Features, models, and plans change. Check Perplexity ↗ and Google's Gemini model catalog ↗.

My short summary

Perplexity is my first try for source-led web discovery; Gemini is my first comparison when Google or multimodal workflows matter. Neither replaces reading primary sources or checking dates and context.
