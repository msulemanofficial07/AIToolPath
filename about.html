Why I built AIToolPath

I am a freelancer and a student. I have spent a long time trying AI tools for different kinds of work, comparing how they fit into actual tasks, and learning that the most popular product is not automatically the right one. I built AIToolPath to share recommendations in my own voice: what I reach for, what I would compare next, and what I still verify myself.

My goal is to help English-speaking readers—especially students, independent professionals, and small teams in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and elsewhere—make informed choices without pretending that one tool can do everything.

What you will find here

  • First-person tool notes based on the author's stated workflow.
  • Task-led comparisons for coding, education, research, writing, and marketing.
  • Clear trade-offs, source links, and reminders to check model access and pricing.
  • Reserved ad locations and, only if added later, disclosed commercial links.

What this site is not

AIToolPath is not an AI model, benchmark lab, provider support channel, or promise of income, rankings, or academic results. My qualitative experience is personal and may not match your account, country, task, or preferences. Product names, terms, prices, and capabilities change.

No public contact address was supplied for this version, so this page does not invent one. If a live site adds a contact method, it should be reviewed and kept current.
