Why I try DeepSeek

As a student and freelancer, cost matters. DeepSeek is worth comparing when its chat service is available to me and I want another option for explanations, brainstorming, coding questions, or reasoning. A free entry point makes experimentation easier, but “free” applies to a specific product, account, location, and time—not every DeepSeek service or unlimited future use.

Free chat is not the same as a free API

Consumer access, rate limits, promotions, and developer/API billing are separate. DeepSeek's official API pricing and model notes ↗ can change. Check the current terms before building an application or estimating recurring use. Also verify availability and terms on DeepSeek's site ↗.

What I would test—and the caveats

I would try DeepSeek on a non-sensitive coding problem, study explanation, or draft that I can review independently. For recent news or policy, use a live-search workflow and original sources. For client work, check data-processing, retention, and privacy terms against the client's requirements.

My short summary

DeepSeek is a compelling low-cost option to try when free access is currently available, especially for low-risk learning and coding experiments. It is not automatically unlimited or free for API use. Verify plan, price, region, and data terms first.
